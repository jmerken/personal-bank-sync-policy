Personal Bank Sync - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Purpose

Personal Bank Sync is a private, non-commercial application used exclusively for managing the application owner's personal financial accounts.

Information Collected

The application retrieves read-only account information, balances and transaction histories through Enable Banking's authorised API.

Data Storage

Financial information is stored locally on systems controlled by the application owner. Banking credentials are not collected or stored by the application.

Data Sharing

Financial data is not sold or used for advertising. Enable Banking and participating financial institutions process information as required to provide authorised access.

Data Retention

Financial records are retained for personal financial management and may be deleted by the application owner.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be directed to the contact email registered with Enable Banking.
