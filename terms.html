Personal Bank Sync - Terms of Use

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Purpose

Personal Bank Sync is a private, non-commercial application developed for the owner's personal financial management.

2. Authorised Use

The application is intended exclusively for accessing financial accounts owned or lawfully controlled by the application owner.

3. Read-Only Access

The application uses authorised Open Banking account information services to retrieve balances, transactions and account details. It does not initiate payments or transfers.

4. Financial Information

Retrieved information is used for personal budgeting, transaction reconciliation and financial analysis. The application does not provide financial or investment advice.

5. Availability

Access to banking information depends on participating financial institutions, Enable Banking, user consent and API availability. Continuous access is not guaranteed.

6. Data Security

Financial information is intended to remain on systems controlled by the application owner. Access credentials and signing keys must be protected against unauthorised disclosure.

7. Changes

These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the application's functionality or operation.

8. Contact

Enquiries may be directed to the contact email registered with Enable Banking.
